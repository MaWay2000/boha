Guide notes and differences from vanilla

38 research entries directly require the Collective starting bonus. Seven more require those entries indirectly and have no other faction gate in their prerequisite chain. The bonus itself is omitted from the prerequisite column. Research points measure lab work. Research power is the listed cost. “Vanilla entry” identifies the same research ID, not necessarily the same effects or prerequisites.

The guide claims a −30% starting power penalty. The archive’s starting bonus contains no power-production modifier for this faction.

  • Longshot Mortar replaces Pepperpot, and Longshot Howitzer replaces Hellstorm in normal Collective research. The Longshot Mortar requires the Collective CB Turret and HE Mortar Shells Mk2. The Longshot Howitzer then requires the Mortar and standard Howitzer.
  • Heavy Scattergun and Blunderbuss fill the late ground machine-gun slots. Scatter AA and Typhoon take over parts of the standard anti-air line.
  • The Collective CB Turret unlock comes from Synaptic Link Data Analysis Mk2 alone. Vanilla also requires Sensor Upgrade and Mortar Targeting Computer. The mod’s guide still lists Mortar Targeting Computer.
  • The nine cyborg thermal armor upgrades retain their vanilla +45 thermal stat increments. Their research IDs carry the Collective faction prerequisite in this archive.
  • Collective and NEXUS share a gate for the nine standard vehicle thermal armor upgrades, Wyvern, Dragon, and Mortar Fast Loader. Other prerequisite chains still apply.
  • The Collective VTOL CB Tower research points to the ordinary VTOL CB Turret entry instead of the Collective variant. The ordinary entry is gated to Project/NEXUS. The Collective Wide Spectrum Sensor uses the Collective VTOL CB Turret, so this tower issue does not block that path.
  • The guide describes a Collective helicopter unlock. The propulsion is marked designable in the archive, with 200 power and speed 400 versus the VTOL’s 150 power and speed 700. There is no helicopter grant in the mod’s setup scripts or research tables, so availability in a normal Collective match is unverified.
  • The archive’s Scattergun, Blunderbuss, Scatter AA and Ripple Rocket stats differ from the guide. The tables use the archive data.

Guide notes and differences from vanilla

All 69 entries below require the starting NEXUS faction bonus. The prerequisites column omits that bonus to make each chain easier to read. Shared and cross-faction changes are listed separately.

The guide claims a −30% starting power penalty. The archive’s starting bonus contains no power-production modifier for this faction.

  • Laser Fortress requires Particle Gun + Plascrete Mk2 in the research data. The guide says Heavy Laser.
  • The mortar defense is named Super Mortar Firebase in the data. Its guide page calls it Tsar Pushka.
  • The guide’s “Gauss Cyborgs Power Up” entry describes Needler and Rail-Gunner. The actual NEXUS Hadron Cyborgs Power Up targets the thermal Laser Cannon and Heavy Beam Gunner weapons.
  • The vehicle-mounted ballistic launcher minimum-range branch reduces MinRange. The corresponding static emplacement entries reduce Radius instead. Both receive the listed short-range accuracy bonus.

Differences from vanilla
  • The +30% power bonus applies at the start. Manufacturing costs 75 power and 2,400 research points and adds 30% production after Engineering.
  • Each Project thermal armor branch has three research steps. Each adds +120% for vehicles and transports or +135% for cyborgs. Vanilla has nine smaller steps per branch. Wyvern and Dragon follow the Project vehicle armor path.

  • Project Ripple Rockets keep the vanilla 86-tile range and eight-round salvo. Collective uses a separate 55-tile, six-round variant. Both factions can research Archangel through separate prerequisites.
