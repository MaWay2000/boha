Camera controls:

  • Drag with mouse2 to rotate
  • Scroll to zoom in or out
  • Use WASD or arrow keys to move
  • Press R to reset the view

Draw on map:

  • Press Space to apply selection
  • Press Esc to cancel selection

Undo / redo:

  • Step back - Ctrl+Z
  • Step forward - Ctrl+Y
About

Warzone2100 MapMaker was created by MaWay2000 for the Warzone 2100 mapmaking community.

The goal is to make editing, previewing, and sharing Warzone 2100 maps easier directly in the browser.
